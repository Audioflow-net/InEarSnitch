InEar SNITCH – How to Open on macOS
=====================================

macOS blocks apps from unknown developers by default.
Here's how to open InEar SNITCH in 30 seconds:

──────────────────────────────────────────────────
METHOD 1 – Quick (Right-Click)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. Drag InEarSnitch.app into your Applications folder
2. RIGHT-CLICK the app icon → select "Open"
3. Click "Open" in the dialog that appears
→ Done! The app opens normally every time after this.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────
METHOD 2 – Via System Settings
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. Drag InEarSnitch.app into your Applications folder
2. Double-click the app (you'll see a warning – that's ok)
3. Open: Apple Menu → System Settings → Privacy & Security
4. Scroll down until you see:
   "InEarSnitch was blocked..."
5. Click "Open Anyway" → confirm
→ Done! The app opens normally every time after this.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────
WHY DOES THIS HAPPEN?
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
InEar SNITCH is in early beta and not yet signed with
an official Apple Developer certificate. This does NOT
mean the app is dangerous – it simply means Apple has
not yet verified the developer identity.
