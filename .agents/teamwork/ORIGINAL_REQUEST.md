An analysis and invention team to design a mechanical decoupling solution for an IEC711 measurement microphone (standing vertically in a 95A TPU tower) inside a Peli 1020 case, isolating it from sub-bass stage rumble without losing the ability to plug/unplug the BNC connector at the bottom. ## Requirements

### R1. Conceptual Decoupling Designs
Develop 2-3 radically different conceptual mechanical solutions to acoustically decouple the vertically mounted IEC711 microphone from 20-100Hz stage rumble.

### R2. Strict Material Constraints
All proposed solutions must be manufacturable using exclusively FDM 3D-printed 95A TPU and custom-cast silicone (cast in 3D-printed molds). No external hardware, commercial springs, or Sorbothane allowed.

### R3. BNC Cable Management
Each solution must explicitly solve the BNC connector contradiction: the connector must be attachable/detachable at the bottom of the tower, and the clamped cable must not act as a structural vibration bridge to the microphone body.

## Acceptance Criteria

### Verification & Viability
- [ ] The final document presents at least 2 distinct mechanical concepts.
- [ ] Every concept includes a step-by-step explanation of how it would be manufactured using only TPU and cast silicone.
- [ ] Every concept includes a dedicated section explaining how the BNC connection is handled without transmitting sub-bass rumble.
