# InEar Snitch Hardware Kit: Package Insert & Safety Instructions

**Please read completely before using the measurement rig (TPU inserts and silicone adapters) for the first time!**

## 1. Liability Disclaimer (IMPORTANT)
**Use the adapters and the measurement rig at your own risk.**  
**Never use force** when inserting In-Ear Monitors into the TPU cradle or the silicone adapters. Custom In-Ear Monitors (CIEMs) are made of sensitive acrylic or UV resin and can crack or scratch if handled improperly or pushed too hard.  
**We are not liable for any mechanical or cosmetic damage to your In-Ear Monitors.** 

This product is a purely mechanical tool for acoustic measurements and is **not a medical device**. It is not intended to be inserted into the human ear canal.

## 2. Material Information & Compatibility
The physical components of the InEar Snitch kit are made of high-quality, industrial materials:

*   **The Case / Cradle (Peli-Insert):**  
    Manufactured from **TPU 95A** (Thermoplastic Polyurethane). This material is highly abrasion-resistant, flexible, and chemically stable.
*   **The Measurement Adapters (Silicone Tips):**  
    Cast from **TFC Silicone Rubber Type 9 (Soft Putty Silicone Shore 25, 1:1)**.  
    This is an *addition-curing* (platinum) silicone. 
    *Why does this matter?* Unlike cheap, acetic acid-curing construction silicones, this silicone does not outgas. It is chemically neutral and **will not** attack the sensitive clear coat or acrylic shell of your expensive Custom In-Ears, leaving no chemical residue.

## 3. Everyday Safety Instructions
*   **No extreme heat:** TPU and silicone can deform when exposed to extreme heat (e.g., in a car during mid-summer). Please store the Peli case at room temperature.
*   **Secure fit:** Before every measurement, ensure that the silicone adapter sits securely and flush on the designated mount (with the sharp 90-degree edge) of the TPU cradle. A slipping adapter can cause the sound nozzle of your IEM to forcefully hit hard plastic.
*   **Cleaning:** The silicone tips and the TPU inlay can be easily cleaned with lukewarm water or a mild glasses cleaning cloth. Do **not** use aggressive solvents (such as acetone or pure isopropanol), as this may dissolve the TPU.
